Active Navigation Link

The link for the page the visitor is on is highlighted: cream background, brown text and a gold bar along the bottom. It is set with the aria-current attribute, so screen readers announce it as well. Use aria-current="page" on the exact page and aria-current="true" for a section the page belongs to (for example, Menu on a product detail page).

CSS selector:

.navBarButtons[aria-current]

Sample code:

<a href="productpage.html" class="navBarButtons" aria-current="page">Menu</a>

Breadcrumbs

Left-aligned trail at the top of every page except the homepage. The last item is the current page and is not a link.

CSS selector:

.breadcrumbs, .breadcrumbsLink, .breadcrumbsButton, .breadcrumbsArrow, .breadcrumbsCurrent

Sample code:

<nav class="breadcrumbs" aria-label="Breadcrumb">
  <div class="breadcrumbsLink">
    <a href="index.html" class="breadcrumbsButton">Home</a>
    <span class="breadcrumbsArrow" aria-hidden="true">&raquo;</span>
    <a href="productpage.html" class="breadcrumbsButton">Menu</a>
    <span class="breadcrumbsArrow" aria-hidden="true">&raquo;</span>
    <span class="breadcrumbsCurrent" aria-current="page">Chocolate Chip Cookie</span>
  </div>
</nav>

Rendered element:

Home » Menu » Chocolate Chip Cookie

Branding

What does the company offer?

  • Sweet and savoury pastries, snacks and food
  • An indoor picnic experience for friends to enjoy together
  • Community events for regulars and people in the neighbourhood, possibly through a membership
  • Customers may bring in outside food, as long as they also purchase from the bakery

Who is the company to potential customers?

If the bakery were a person, it would be a welcoming host who makes everyone feel at home.

  • A chill spot to hang out with friends
  • A place to try new food and snacks
  • A study, work or date spot

Why might someone purchase from this company?

To make a first purchase

  • A snack
  • Friendly customer service
  • A comfortable, inviting environment

To keep coming back

  • A sense of relationship and belonging
  • In-store exclusive events for members
